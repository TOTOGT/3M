Theorem B.4: Jacobian Entry J₂₂ = r_s²

Statement

Theorem B.4 — Exact Jacobian Entry J₂₂
At the saddle equilibrium (rs, zs):
J22|saddle = rs²
The (2,2) entry of the Jacobian at the saddle equals the square of the saddle r-coordinate. ✓ Lean 4 verified (0 sorrys)

Proof

The (2,2) entry of the Jacobian is the partial derivative of ż with respect to z:

J22 = ∂ż/∂z = ∂/∂z [r² − 2(r−1)²e−z] = 2(r−1)²e−z

At any equilibrium, ż = 0 requires:

r² − 2(r−1)²e−z = 0   ⟺   2(r−1)²e−z = r²

Therefore at the saddle:

J22|saddle = 2(rs−1)²e−zs = rs²   ★ □

This proof uses only the equilibrium condition — no knowledge of rs's explicit value is required. It holds for any ε ≥ 0.

Literature Gap Closed

Structural observation: J22 = rs² is immediate from the equilibrium condition and holds at any ε. It is the building block for both the trace formula (B.3) and the eigenvalue computation (B.5). Its Lean 4 verification is the only part of the saddle geometry package that is currently machine-checked end-to-end.

Physical Consequence

Vertical dynamics: J22 = rs² ≈ 0.198 measures how perturbations in z evolve near the saddle. Since 0 < rs² < 1, the z-direction is locally contracting at the saddle — perturbations in z decay. The saddle's instability is therefore entirely in the r-direction, driven by the positive eigenvalue λ+ of Theorem B.5.
